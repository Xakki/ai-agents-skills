# Epic finalization

Apply only after all required children have accepted evidence and the owner authorizes coordinated completion. Re-read the parent, exact declared children, current stages and authorized order; do not move unrelated release blockers. If cards are already accepted in `done`, verify rather than replay them.

Review the complete candidate including relevant uncommitted and untracked shipped files. Recover the complete verdict and blockers, preserve authentic prior gate results, and distinguish accepted implementation from pending authorized Git predicates. Reuse unchanged evidence where valid; do not describe a clarification as a newly executed test.

Append sanitized parent evidence stating reviewed source, gates, boundaries and history. Validate active cards before transitions if the linter excludes archived stages, preserving actual checked counts. Use the canonical mover for only authorized parent and child transitions; verify exact destinations, unique IDs and absence of active duplicates. A zero-card lint is not validation.

For an authorized commit, stage only intended final destination bodies and inspect the complete cached paths and contents, including renames. Follow the repository's message policy; verify exit, SHA, commit contents, source pins and relevant worktree/index state. Merge, branch archival, push and live release remain separate actions governed by their own authorizations. Keep secrets out of evidence.
