# Resume and career evidence

Use when asked to convert a resume, portfolio or other supplied evidence into a profile, competency matrix or questionnaire. Identify the authoritative source documents, actual form, requested language, output scope and permitted edits. Separate employment, contract work, open source, personal experiments and supported project evidence; do not imply production experience or a duration the evidence does not establish.

Map each answer to relevant evidence and the form's exact meaning. Surface uncertain or missing facts instead of inventing dates, ratings, employers, achievements or technologies. When duration and seniority are requested, avoid double-counting overlapping periods and explain assumptions.

Prepare a reviewable draft or edit the explicitly authorized target through a supported interface. For an actual write, verify the saved fields and report any gaps; do not send applications, messages or share personal data outside the task's authorized scope.
