# Decision recording

Establish the decision owner, the current contract and source of authority, alternatives, the effect of each choice, and the exact acceptance boundary. Do not merge independently decidable choices into one approval or treat a capability, credential, or related decision as authorization for a broader operation.

After an explicit answer, update the canonical card and authorized dependents. Preserve unresolved questions, mark superseded statements where readers encounter them, and verify that scope, positive and negative conditions, dependency order, and observable acceptance remain consistent. Silence, timeout, or a continuation request does not decide an unanswered question.

For security-sensitive data or live operations, include resource scope, reader/writer authority, failure and recovery behavior, permitted effects, and measurable verification. Keep any technology-specific recipes in the project or an appropriate conditional reference rather than making one deployment universal.
