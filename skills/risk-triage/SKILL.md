# Risk triage

Separate observed incident, reproduced defect, plausible but unverified risk and unsupported hypothetical. Identify the affected asset, actor, trust boundary, trigger, consequence, likelihood evidence, current protections and relevant contractual requirements. Supported concurrency, external access and authenticated operator actions must be assessed on their actual facts, not dismissed as irrational.

Check whether the scenario is in the current task, affects acceptance or requires a separate decision. Compare proportionate mitigation, verification, documented deferral and no action; include prerequisites and tradeoffs. Do not silently downgrade severity, move a board card, waive a required check or expand scope. If the owner decides to defer, capture the evidence and reactivation condition through the authorized workflow.
