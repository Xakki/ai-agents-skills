# Brand naming

Define the audience, intended meaning, languages and jurisdictions, exclusions, pronunciation needs, current product scope and plausible future extensions. Generate a manageable variety of descriptive, suggestive and invented directions where useful; distinguish substantive differences from spelling variants.

Assess candidates against the brief: pronunciation, spelling, memorability, unwanted associations, similarity to existing names and operational fit. Treat sound symbolism, fixed syllable counts and naming frameworks as optional heuristics, not measured laws or proof of market performance. Provide concise rationale and trade-offs; do not invent a launch outcome or quantitative benefit.

If requested, check domains and existing uses through available authoritative, current sources. State exactly what was checked, where and when. Domain availability does not establish trademark clearance, and preliminary search is not legal advice or a definitive registry opinion. Do not buy, register or contact third parties without the user's authorization.
