# Historical lifecycle wording

Search the entire card for claims that it remains in an earlier stage, must not advance, is awaiting approval, still has open questions, or has completed rollout or handoff. Compare each claim to current decisions and evidence.

If a later decision resolves an older statement, recommend removing redundant text or labeling it as superseded with a reference to the resolving decision. Do not erase future acceptance gates to make a card appear complete. `ready` describes the board's actual handoff contract and does not by itself establish production, rollback, or operational acceptance.

Record the current canonical path and maintained status, duplicate search, contradictory wording with location, resolving evidence, validator result or reason unavailable, and the final source snapshot.
